The three already-used PDFs (two *Actividades de diptongos e hiatos* files and *Ficha_Pronombres_Personales_5_Primaria*) are not imported as duplicate exercises. Existing question IDs and topic IDs remain stable, preserving recorded history. New questions append to the bank.

Open drawing, writing and discussion tasks are adapted into single-answer questions so automated correction is reliable. Questions always provide the context needed to choose a single answer. Reading answers are explicitly based on the supplied text, not presented as general scientific conclusions. Modern classroom terms are used alongside equivalents where helpful (desiderativa/optativa, sujeto omitido).

All sets support shuffled questions/options, immediate explanations, a score and error review. Mixed review includes at least one question from every available topic, with remaining places sampled across the bank. Cloud validation and import/export accept all added topic IDs.

Valencian subject questions and explanations are in Valencian. The common navigation remains Spanish. Topic IDs are namespaced (`val-…`, `math-…`) so shared history and mixed reviews remain separate by subject. Numeric answer keys are calculated and tested independently. Large integers use dots as thousands separators.

Editorial corrections: the supplied Valencian review sheet contradicts itself about **tx**; the app uses **cot-xe**, following standard syllabic separation. It also does not treat *melons* as requiring an accent: it ends in a consonant cluster (-ns), not in vowel + s. Basic diphthong examples avoid applying Spanish syllabification rules to Valencian. Reference: Acadèmia Valenciana de la Llengua, *Gramàtica valenciana bàsica*, orthographic syllable separation: https://alzira.es/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/4_GVB.pdf .

Current totals: 351 Castellano, 91 Matemáticas, and 102 Valencià questions; 23 individual sets and three subject-specific mixed reviews. No PDFs or source archives are served publicly.
